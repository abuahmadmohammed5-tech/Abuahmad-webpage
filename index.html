About the Hub

The Coast Youth Enterprise, Leadership & Maritime Innovation Hub is a platform designed to support young people in coastal communities by providing useful information, learning opportunities and ideas for personal and professional development.

The Hub encourages young people to develop entrepreneurial ideas, leadership skills, sustainable solutions and innovations related to maritime transport and logistics.

Training Areas

Entrepreneurship

Learn about business ideas, enterprise development and opportunities for young entrepreneurs.

Leadership

Develop teamwork, responsibility, communication and leadership skills.

Sustainability

Explore environmentally responsible ideas that can benefit coastal communities.

Operations Management

Understand basic ideas about planning, organizing and managing activities effectively.

Maritime Innovation

Discover ideas and opportunities related to maritime transport, logistics and innovation.
