ABOUT THE HUB

Empowering Coastal Youth

The Coast Youth Enterprise, Leadership & Maritime Innovation Hub is a youth-focused platform designed to bring learning, entrepreneurship and innovation opportunities closer to young people in coastal communities.

The Hub focuses on developing practical skills in entrepreneurship, leadership, sustainability, operations management and maritime innovation. It encourages young people to learn, create ideas, work together and contribute positively to their communities.

Gain useful knowledge and practical skills for personal and professional development.
